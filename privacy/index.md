# S4 Business — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 9, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

S4 Business is a social media management and automation platform designed to help businesses create, organize, schedule, approve, and publish content across supported social media platforms.

S4 Business is currently under development and undergoing limited testing.

This Privacy Policy explains the information S4 Business may collect and process, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, S4 Business may process:

- Business names, contact information, and business profile details.
- Social media account identifiers, usernames, and profile information authorized through connected platforms.
- Content uploaded or created by users, including images, videos, captions, hashtags, and scheduled posts.
- Publishing status, activity history, and API error information.
- Authentication information and access tokens required to maintain authorized social media connections.
- Technical information needed for application security, troubleshooting, and operation.

We seek to collect only information necessary to provide the requested features.

## 3. Connected Social Media Platforms

S4 Business is designed to integrate with third-party platforms, including TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, and Threads.

When users connect an account, the relevant platform presents an authorization process identifying the permissions requested.

Depending on the platform and permissions granted, S4 Business may access basic profile information, prepare content for publication, upload drafts, or publish content at the user's direction.

S4 Business does not require users to provide their social media passwords directly.

Users may revoke permissions through the connected platform's account settings.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Connect and manage authorized social media accounts.
- Create, organize, schedule, and publish content.
- Maintain content approval workflows.
- Display publishing history and account connection status.
- Diagnose technical problems and improve service reliability.
- Protect accounts and prevent unauthorized use.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

S4 Business does not intend to sell personal information or use connected social media data for unrelated advertising.

## 5. TikTok Integration

S4 Business uses TikTok's official authorization and Content Posting APIs where permitted.

With appropriate authorization, the application may access basic TikTok account information and upload or publish content requested by the account owner.

TikTok account access is subject to TikTok's permissions, developer policies, and API restrictions.

Users can revoke S4 Business access through their TikTok account settings.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

S4 Business is being designed to store business data and authorized account connection information within its backend infrastructure.

The intended security controls include restricted access to credentials, protection of authentication tokens, and separation of data between business accounts.

Because the application is still under development, these controls must be implemented and verified before general public availability.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Sharing

S4 Business may transmit authorized content and necessary account information to connected social media platforms to perform user-requested actions.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure providers necessary to operate the application.

We do not intend to sell connected account data to third parties.

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

## 8. Data Retention

Information will be retained only as reasonably necessary for the relevant service purpose, legitimate operational needs, or applicable legal requirements.

Specific retention periods and automated deletion procedures will be finalized before public launch.

## 9. Account Disconnection and Data Deletion

Users can revoke social media permissions through the relevant platform.

Users may request deletion of personal information or connected account information by contacting S4 Business using the contact details below.

Deletion requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law, subject to legitimate retention obligations.

Revoking platform permissions does not necessarily remove information already stored by S4 Business.

## 10. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below.

## 11. Children's Privacy

S4 Business is intended for business users and is not designed for children under 13.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

## 12. International Data Processing

Information may be processed in locations where S4 Business or its infrastructure providers operate, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as S4 Business develops, new features are introduced, or legal requirements change.

The updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.
