# S4 Business

**Social Media Management and Automation for Small Businesses**

S4 Business is a web-based platform being developed to help small businesses manage their social media presence from one place.

## What S4 Business Does

- **Content Management:** Create and organize social media posts, captions, images, and videos.
- **Content Scheduling:** Plan posts using a centralized publishing calendar.
- **Approval Workflow:** Review and approve content before publication.
- **Social Account Connections:** Connect authorized business accounts through supported platform integrations.
- **Automated Publishing:** Publish approved content to supported social media platforms, subject to platform permissions and availability.
- **Publishing History:** Track posting activity and publication status.

## Supported Platform Integrations

S4 Business is developing integrations for:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- Threads
- TikTok

Integration availability depends on platform authorization, testing, and approval.

## Development Status

S4 Business is currently under active development and limited testing. The full web application is not yet publicly available.

Golden Brows Threading & Beauty Studio is being used as the initial business testing account.
